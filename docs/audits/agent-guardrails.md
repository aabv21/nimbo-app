**Statuses.** As in [`security.md`](security.md): `fixed` names its test; `accepted` gives the reason and the cost (a
control that held under a new test is `accepted`, with that test); `deferred` needs a live account or the real models
and is listed under "Deferred live checks".

**Method.** Each layer was read in the code, then attacked through the function with a fake `fetch` and a scripted
model (`supabase/functions/companion/__tests__/redTeam.test.ts`) and on the local stack with the mocks
(`integration/redTeam.int.test.ts`). The tests were written first; they failed only on AG-01, AG-07, AG-08, RT-03 and
RT-05 (a round of 12 calls ran all 12; a note's and a dose text's zero-width and bidi characters reached Claude; a
Markdown link left debris and bare domains passed; 12 evasion positives were missed), and on AG-05's new state field.

## Known residuals

- Two one-letter words right before a spelled-out one ("e a n u c a"), a word spelled with two letters, and look-alike
  letters from other scripts (a Cyrillic "о" in "wоrst") are not folded: the model screen must catch those, and with the
  AI off they get no urgent-care screen.
- A bare domain with an unlisted top-level domain and no query ("evil.zz/x") stays in a reply (RT-05).
- The real Jev and Haiku have not judged the new fixtures or the `earlier_user_messages` wording yet.
