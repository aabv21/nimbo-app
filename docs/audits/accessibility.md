| Scope | The app under `src/` at `a00d4da` (branch `feat/hardening`): the tab bar, large text on Today, crisis mode, History and Patterns, the companion, Profile, the boundary, export and delete screens; contrast of the Day, Dark and Dim tokens; touch targets; screen-reader roles, names, state and announcements; the head map; reduce motion. |

**Statuses.** `fixed`: the code changed here, or a missing regression test was added, and the row names the test.
`accepted`: the control holds or the gap is taken on purpose; the row gives the reason and the cost. `deferred`: it needs
a device with a screen reader or a large text setting, and is listed under "Deferred live checks".

**Method.** Two source scans (`src/__tests__/a11yScan.test.ts`) and a contrast test over every token pair
(`src/ui/__tests__/contrast.test.ts`), then a read of each scope area against the skill's rule groups (names, semantics,
forms and errors, announcements, contrast and states, motion). Jest renders no layout, so wrapping is checked through the
styles that allow it (`flexShrink`, no `numberOfLines`, no fixed height) and judged on a device in the owner session.

## Deferred live checks

- TalkBack (and VoiceOver once iOS unparks) through onboarding, crisis mode, the companion, export and delete: each control's name, role and state, the reading order (the head map's included), that an error notice and an inline error are spoken once when they appear, and that standing notices (overuse, sync failure, consent updated or paused) are read in their turn rather than announced over the screen.
- Font scale 200 % on the same screens plus Today, History (list, calendar and Patterns) and Profile: nothing truncated or clipped, chips wrap, the tab labels ("Histórico", "Historial" on a narrow phone) shrink to fit on one line without an ellipsis, the tab bar grows and the last row still scrolls clear of it.
- Reduce motion on (the OS setting, then the in-app switch): every change is a 150 ms cross-fade, nothing moves or staggers.
